Attendance policy: Students must attend at least 80 percent of scheduled classes for each course. Instructors may mark a student ineligible for the final exam if attendance falls below this requirement.

Grading policy: Letter grades are assigned from A to F. A passing grade requires meeting the course learning outcomes and completing required assessments.

Academic integrity policy: Plagiarism, unauthorized collaboration, and cheating are violations. Cases may be referred to the disciplinary committee.

Add/drop policy: Students may add or drop courses during the official enrollment window announced by the registrar.

Financial aid policy: Need-based financial aid is reviewed annually and may require updated family income documentation.
